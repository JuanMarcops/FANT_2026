On 4 September 2026, researchers in the archaeology of Africa will gather in Frankfurt. The aim is to maintain the network established through the SPP "Entangled Africa", to foster new connections, and to promote research in African archaeology. There will be 3 sessions with presentations by various researchers. Finally, we will come together for a joint roundtable on the topic of archaeometry in African archaeology.
