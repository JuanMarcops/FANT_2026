The event takes place on the Westend Campus, in the annex building (Nebengebäude) of the IG-Farben-Haus (Poelzig-Bau), room NG 1.741a, 1st floor.

The annex building is right next to the IG-Farben-Haus: from its main entrance, keep to the back/right, and the annex building is signposted from there.

If you're coming from the Holzhausenstraße U-Bahn stop or getting off at the "Bremerstraße" bus stop, the annex building is directly behind the entrance to the grounds.
